# Route 2: Stock Capture Protocol

## Objective
To capture a property's geometric and visual data efficiently and reliably without requiring specialized engineering hardware, utilizing standard consumer mobile devices.

## App Selection
* **LiDAR Tier:** Record3D (Available on iOS App Store)
* **Video Tier:** Native Apple Camera (4K 30fps)
* **Photo Tier:** Native Camera (24MP stills)

## Walk Path Protocol
1. **Initial Stance:** Hold the phone at chest height (approximately 1.3 meters).
2. **Device Angle:** Tilt the phone 10° downwards towards the floor to ensure sufficient feature tracking on the ground surface.
3. **Movement:** Walk the perimeter of the room in a clockwise direction.
4. **Pacing:** Maintain a steady walking speed of approximately 0.5 meters per second (m/s).
5. **Doorways:** Ensure overlapping views of doorways and connecting spaces when transitioning between rooms.
6. **Loop Closure (LiDAR specifically):** Always ensure loop completion by returning to the exact origin point of the capture within the room or overall path.
